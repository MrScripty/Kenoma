Drag the round hand/foot targets and diamond elbow/knee poles. Drag the root to move a character. Select Turn character or Turn head to use rotation rings. The arrows constrain movement to an axis.

Orbit: drag empty space · Pan: right drag or two fingers · Zoom: scroll or pinch
Focused scene: arrow keys move the selected handle (Alt + ↑/↓: depth). Rotation controls: ←/→ turn; ↑/↓ tilt the head. Shift: fine steps.
F: frame · Delete: remove · Ctrl/⌘ Z: undo · Shift Ctrl/⌘ Z: redo · Esc: cancel drag
