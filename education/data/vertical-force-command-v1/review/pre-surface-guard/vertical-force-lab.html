Model and validation scope

OpenSim 4.5.2 source curves; educational peak force 100 N, fiber optimum 0.1 m and tendon slack length 0.2 m. The added force controller uses the preregistered continuous-time gains 0.4 and 8/s with excitation limits and conditional integration. This is a separate scalar implementation, not the full OpenSim runtime or a human arm.

Force-only PI cannot stabilize a negative-static-stiffness free-mass equilibrium. Zero and growing modes are retained. Matching force to weight does not stop existing velocity. Excitation-floor release leaves activation and stored tendon force; it does not detach the load. Curves retain their source extrapolations.

Numerical completion, target tracking and motion are different outcomes. Browser consistency checks are not an independent replay of every custom input. Invalid stages and unqualified slack/lower-length events stop at the last accepted state. Mass changes restart the experiment.
