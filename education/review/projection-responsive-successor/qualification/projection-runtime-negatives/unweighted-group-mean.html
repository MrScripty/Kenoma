The projection is explicit
(Pg)ᵢ = Σⱼ∈Gᵢ wⱼ gⱼ / Σⱼ∈Gᵢ wⱼ

Gᵢ is the selected group containing sample i. Q consists exactly of vectors constant on each group. Positive weights make each group denominator positive.

Why this is orthogonal
Σⱼ∈G wⱼ (gⱼ − (Pg)ⱼ) = 0

The unresolved part has zero weighted sum in each group. It is therefore weighted-orthogonal to every group-constant vector in Q. Ordinary unweighted averages would give a different projector.

Interpretation and limits

g is prescribed, not solved. Interpreting its entries as log J requires positive J and justified sampling; no continuum or incompressibility result follows. K is one positive constant. The Lean projector is assumed exact. Pressure-vector uniqueness does not certify arbitrary basis-coefficient uniqueness, which needs injective evaluation. No nonlinear equilibrium, inf-sup stability, derivative/Hessian gap, anatomical validity, or floating-point refinement proof is claimed.
