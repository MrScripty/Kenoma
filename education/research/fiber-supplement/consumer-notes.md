Before any future mixture is used in mechanics, specify the measured quantity and the mapping assumptions. If count fractions are `c_i` and measured mean fiber areas are `A_i`, a representative sampled cross-section has area weights `w_i = c_i A_i / Σ_j c_j A_j`; this requires comparable sampling and complete population coverage, including hybrids. MHC-protein fractions do not determine those weights without additional assumptions about protein concentration, fiber size and sampled tissue.

Under an explicitly assumed parallel, common-strain population, a possible tendon-force model is `F = PCSA Σ_i w_i σ_i(a_i, λ_i, v_i) cos(α_i)`, with PCSA in square metres, stress in pascals and force in newtons. Architecture, activation, force–length/velocity laws and the area-weight definition require separate evidence. This is an assumption to evaluate, not an implemented or calibrated law. Do not multiply the current full-muscle fixed-end force target by a protein percentage and call it a measured type-specific force.
