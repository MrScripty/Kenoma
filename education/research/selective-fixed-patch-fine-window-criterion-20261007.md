**Proposal only. No new material calls, quadrature generation, runner change,
execution authorization or publication.** This does not alter the completed
result `38ae8a2e2af57cf33254af987b724824b9d84357`, its gates or any failed

The [read-only diagnosis](../review/selective-fixed-patch-arithmetic-review-20261007/README.md)
identifies secondary outer-shell angular sensitivity. Q0's secondary C55

## Why a new criterion is legitimate

A coarse rule is allowed to remain coarse forever. Its immutable disagreement
with a better-resolved rule cannot change after a finer calculation. Requiring
that same historical pair to pass in every later protocol makes acceptance
impossible by construction and tests the coarse rule rather than the proposed
fine rule. The completed protocol deliberately used that strong coarse-pair
requirement and failed it; its verdict is preserved.

A separately named, prospectively frozen protocol can instead assess a fine
resolution window. This is justified by targeted new resolution evidence,
not by deleting a failed comparison or raising a tolerance. Choose recipes,
fields, partitions, acceptance window, all comparisons and budgets **before**
new evaluations; obtain independent structural review and separate parent
execution acceptance. Keep every historical and newly failed result visible.
No arbitrary stopping at the first favorable pair or post-result relabeling.

## Recommended minimum evidence

1. **Primary fine sequence.** Predeclare three sufficiently fine primary levels
   S0/S1/S2 with genuine refinement in the suspected angular direction, fixed
   other axes/chart for that sequence, and nonidentical sampled rules. Require
   S0/S1 and S1/S2 both below the unchanged force/work gates for every component
   at both fixed fields. The current Q1 may supply S0; immutable Q0 is retained
   as the earlier failed coarse diagnostic, not a required future passing pair.
   Point count alone is not a resolution witness. If angular increments pass
   only after another level, that requires a separately predeclared window and
   budget rather than a post-hoc relaxation. Report successive differences on
   a matched partition, including any decreases or increases. Claim an empirically improving sequence only when the
   reduction exceeds independently audited binary64 reduction noise. Gaussian
   errors need not be monotone, so a small nonmonotone fine increment is not by
   itself proof of failure or convergence; the prescribed fine-increment and
   independent cross-family gates still apply. Do not impose an invented
   convergence order or use Richardson extrapolation without a demonstrated
   asymptotic model.
2. **Independent resolved family.** Predeclare at least two sufficiently fine
   levels I0/I1 of a genuinely independent sampling family and require their
   own fine-increment agreement. A uniform Duffy or graded-tetrahedral family
   would need its relevant spatial/order resolution demonstrated; calling an
   old high-point-count rule independent does not establish that it is resolved.
   Reuse an existing witness only when its fields/law/support/recipe/partition
   identities and adequacy are explicitly defended. The retained secondary
   D4/D5 witness is useful, but its same spatial grid and the unresolved U5/D5
   triangle prevent treating it as an absolute-error certificate.
3. **Finest cross-family comparison.** Require S2/I1 agreement for the complete
   16-element patch, all five terms, both fields and all585 nodes including held
   reactions. No patch element may disappear. Whole-element differences are
   legitimate where an old family retained only whole-element forces; never
   fabricate a shell partition. Where new independent sampling supplies
   genuinely recorded matching physical-region contributions, keep those too.
   An alternate face permutation in the same shell family is a useful chart
   check, not a replacement for an independently resolved quadrature family.
4. **Separate resolution axes.** On the affected elements, independently vary
   radial, angular and core-depth resolutions and face chart at a fine working
   resolution. Hold the other choices fixed for each sensitivity check; preserve
   all original regions before any common-bin grouping. Current F44/R44 supplies
   only247's radial witness at angular4; it says nothing new about the other15
   elements. Current F44/X44 couples depth and chart and cannot isolate either.
   Large new axis sensitivity rejects the candidate regardless of favorable
   other comparisons. Keep complete positive reference-weight coverage and
   the exact cap/J guards and moment/source checks.

These are finite numerical resolution checks, not proof of convergence to the
exact integral. A smaller Q1/new-finer/Q2 set can establish a weaker, explicitly
named bounded finite-agreement result only if its independent-family resolution
witnesses, complete support and budgets are defended in advance. By itself it
contains only one primary increment and a mixed cross-check; it cannot justify
calling the entire patch numerically converged.

## Exact unchanged acceptance metrics

For each state and component t, compare the same disjoint physical comparison
units u. Scatter each unit difference d(u,t) onto all585×3 entries. Require

- signed force: `max(abs(sum_u d(u,t))) <= 1e-5 N`;
- unit triangle: `max(sum_u abs(d(u,t))) <= 1e-5 N`;
- signed saved-direction work: `abs(sum_u dot(d(u,t),v)) <= 5.492029235357012e-7 J`;
- unit-work triangle: `sum_u abs(dot(d(u,t),v)) <= 5.492029235357012e-7 J`.

Use a stable partition for every comparison claimed to show shrinking
refinement differences. Supplement whole-element cross-family comparisons
with shell-resolved primary refinement checks to expose cancellation within
an element. Do not compare a156-unit triangle to a36-unit triangle as if they
were identical error estimators. The current diagnosis also provides a common
16-whole-element diagnostic; it does not replace the completed protocol's gates.
Within-unit cancellation can still occur, and work bounds follow algebraically
from forces and the frozen direction; neither is an analytic quadrature bound.

Retained quiet-element and other already-measured witnesses may be reused
without new calls, but identical reused contributions contribute no independent
new resolution evidence. Attribute their prior independent comparisons explicitly.
For every composite witness, form the actual complete16 scatter and unit triangle.
If grouping is used to budget distinct witnesses, require allocated force and
work budgets whose sums do not exceed the same global gates; do not give each
subset a fresh full global budget and then combine them unchecked. Reuse the
baseline once, avoid shared-node double counting, and retain energy/force
component and region/element/global reconstructions at1e-8N/1e-9J.

The physical stationarity gate stays1e-4N and is separate from integration
agreement. Keep the frozen fields, direction with held entries exactly zero,
material/activation, reference configuration and weighting convention unchanged.
No optimizer, refit or additional nodal field is part of this criterion.

## Separate disposition and limits

A possible new result name is `PASS_BOUNDED_FINE_WINDOW_FIXED_PATCH_AGREEMENT`;
its exact schema and runner would require future review. A pass would concern
only that new fine-window protocol. The original result and Q0/Q1 failures stay
unchanged. Any missing independent witness, failed fine increment, new axis
sensitivity, recon failure, resource refusal or incomplete execution remains
unresolved/incomplete in that new protocol. Every new rule has an exact bounded
call/storage/wall/memory budget and fail-closed receipts before execution.

The236 outside elements retain the old U3 baseline and remain unqualified;
zero differences from reusing them cannot validate their integration. Even a
future16-patch pass does not establish whole-body integration, continuum or
analytic error bounds, equilibrium, tangent, displacement or anatomical
acceptance. Those tasks require additional independently justified work.
