# Research-book teaching acceptance inventory

Feature scope: 27 canonical chapters, 95 executable worked checkpoints. Each checkpoint prescribes bounded adapter inputs and describes an observable result. Desktop uses chapter navigation, readable text and one sticky laboratory. Phones use chapter and example panels; the example remains beside a visible reading area when expanded. Manual control, keyboard focus or camera input pauses reading synchronization until the reader resumes it.

The source chapters, citations, 115 Lean declarations, original scientific kernels and recorded evidence are unchanged. The independent poser uses its existing scene/IK/skin contract. New adapter readouts and illustrations are not implementation proofs or calibrated anatomical predictions.

## Execution classification

This inventory classifies the **new embedded chapter panels**, not every standalone laboratory, figure or historical experiment linked by the book. It follows the actual dispatch in [chapter-models.mjs](../web/chapter-models.mjs), its [worker](../web/chapter-worker.mjs), and the [asset/poser loader](../web/chapter-examples.mjs). Source inspection finds **5 live analytic panels, 3 prescribed-geometry panels, 9 live solver/integration panels, 3 poser panels, 1 saved numerical replay, and 6 stored-reference/mixed provenance panels**. These categories total 27; they are execution categories, not levels of scientific validity.

“Live” means that the selected controls call the existing equations or numerical algorithm in the browser. The oscillator and hinge rebuild their requested bounded trajectory from declared initial conditions; changing their step count does not continue an arbitrary prior manual state. The dissipative bar computes each force/extension protocol once per worker and hold mode, then scrubs that in-memory trace. Its readout named “Saved protocol time” does **not** refer to an archived experiment JSON. The material, serial, tendon, continuum and spatial panels recompute bounded static/single-step results, including unconverged results. No iteration budget is treated as acceptance.

Prescribed geometry evaluates and measures an imposed map without solving for forces. The independent poser does execute IK and geometric skinning, but supplies no constitutive law, contact response or biomechanical result. Recorded numerical replay selects unchanged stored values; drawing a current camera view, rigid attachment or labelled mounting guide does not rerun its solver. Stored atlas identity and successful UI interaction likewise do not establish calibration.

## Remaining scientific qualification gaps

- **Calibration:** authored actuator parameters, tendon areas/routes, matrix/fibre laws and rigid-body assumptions remain model choices. Matching independent Arm26 force targets is not a fit to the atlas subject or OpenArm participant. The [frozen calibration nodal diagnostic](../data/anatomical-arm-v1/audit/anatomical-calibration-nodal.json) retains maximum free nodal components of approximately 64.07, 52.06 and 54.22 N in the three inspected flexor states; a small retained-mode residual does not establish full nodal stationarity.
- **Contact and coupling:** the tendon chapter's block uses an authored plate gap and homogeneous lateral solve. The spatial network has finite compliant contact samples and one-way prescribed-pose coupling; its nonzero penetration remains visible. The poser has no contact response, and the saved synthetic coupled fixture has no tissue-contact model. Later anatomical finite surface/path/sample checks do not establish continuous-motion contact, friction, finite-radius tendon clearance or a measured pressure field.
- **Refinement and local response:** the later dense receipt documents 15 accepted reduced-model increments through 0.43 s, with minimum queried corner J about 0.712025. Separately, [2048-point frozen integration](../data/anatomical-arm-v1/audit/anatomical-further-integration.json) at 0.10 s reports 0.017385 N residual against the unchanged 0.0001 N gate and fails that gate. These distinct experiments do not qualify full nodal, mesh, quadrature or timestep convergence. Near-unit total volume does not imply local incompressibility; increased resolution may expose greater local compression.
- **Evidence/proof transfer:** UI/browser tests establish the exercised control and rendering behavior. Existing Lean declarations retain their stated assumptions and source-bound receipts; neither those declarations nor archived numerical success flags prove JavaScript refinement, an unimplemented interface exercise or clinical validity. Current-source release/PDF/proof qualification belongs to the separate build gates below.

## Review and qualification boundary

`tools/build_teaching_preview.py` reconstructs the checksum-verified preserved publication from two pinned repository snapshots, fetching those commits from the existing official repository when they are absent locally. `--offline` requires both snapshots to be available already; `--preserved` accepts an existing reconstructed publication. The wrapper checks the pinned build manifest, original executable hashes, unchanged chapter/proof source hashes and unchanged poser source/WASM hashes, then assembles current worked readings and UI. It runs no physical campaign or new proof compilation and does not publish. Old build/browser receipts are moved under `preserved-evidence`; the preview has its own source and executable manifest. The linked PDF is explicitly labelled as the preserved prior book.

The normal `npm run build` path also assembles these sequences into HTML and static Markdown. It retains the existing fresh proof and numerical gates. No new release-qualified book or new PDF is claimed by the preview. Fresh pinned Lean/Rust compilation, full book/PDF qualification and an exact-head remote workflow remain required before an approved merge/publication. No main merge, Pages deployment, PR15 change, new medical dataset or license change belongs to this feature.
