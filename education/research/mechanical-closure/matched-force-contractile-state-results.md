# Matched-force contractile state: bounded result

**Carried state supplies a positive fast response at unchanged initial force, but does not remove the original relaxed negative direction.** This is a qualified material-point experiment and frozen-block endpoint response projection. It is not a new nonlinear block or anatomical trajectory. The [pre-execution protocol](matched-force-contractile-state-protocol.md) was committed and pushed before implementation; the source was committed and tested before the numerical coupling run. ## Matched response and refinement

The experiment reuses **24 already qualified nonlinear density histories**, selecting R3/pCa4.5, three node counts, four signed displacements and two time resolutions. It reads carried first-jump/hold states, rather than advancing another ODE or resetting state. An independently derived reaction Jacobian and symmetric matrix exponential provide the endpoint tangent. A separate direct nonsymmetric `expm` regression verifies the similarity construction. Reaction-action finite differences, exact initial transport differentiation and the finite-domain endpoint correction are checked separately. The correction is only 2.18e-22 in the phi derivative and is explicitly retained in the audit.

A second implementation using globally scattered strain-gradient rows and a Gram product reproduces all seven sampled operator spectra to within 2.13e-11 N/m and witness responses within 2.91e-11 N/m.

These are **powered endpoint response operators**, not Hessians of passive stored energy and not dynamical stability certificates. The fixed-transverse point includes a bulk response that makes its total tangent positive even at .2 s and in the relaxed limit. That constrained point result does not establish positive full-block response. No continuum spectral convergence is asserted.

All **480 fixed checks pass**. The worst central-difference endpoint tangent error is 7.39415e-7 relative, under the unchanged 1e-4 derivative gate. Halving link displacement reduces the maximum error to approximately 1.84872e-7. Both amplitudes, both time resolutions and all three node counts are checked at the same six first-hold endpoints. The original strict population, initial residual/adaptive-integral and exact jump gates pass without changed tolerance or extra iteration. These checks qualify this bounded coupling's response; they do not qualify full anatomical nodal/quadrature/timestep convergence.

## Energy accounting

The active subsystem is explicitly powered. At the matched stationary state, attachment supplies approximately **.5398668064 W** of link elastic input and detachment removes the same amount, while link storage is constant. Neither channel is a complete ATP/heat model. Link storage is reported separately from passive matrix/fiber/bulk stored energy, and no passive dissipation law is invented.

For the +.001 link jump, active mechanical work is approximately 3.75759e-5 J, signed envelope/recruitment work −8.27803e-7 J and active link storage change 3.67481e-5 J. Direct endpoint detachment removal is approximately 1.62e-26 J. The jump identity includes the envelope work; silently dropping it would break the declared energy accounting. This is idealized full-support transport followed by endpoint detachment, not a continuous moving-boundary reaction path. The hold records separately report attachment, detachment removal and link-storage rate from the actual carried nonlinear states. Maximum new passive work identity error is 1.65e-13 Pa after division by reference volume, under the predeclared 2e-12 gate. No global thermodynamic or passivity claim follows.

The combination of rat 22°C kinetics and human 15°C mean amplitude is educational, not matched-specimen calibration. The multiplicative macro envelope coupling is new and declared, rather than claimed as the original article's full constitutive law.

The constitutive question is answered within this protocol: simply adding a transported two-state strain density at matched initial force provides fast stiffness but leaves the relaxed descending-envelope issue. A physically supported overlap/recruitment or richer contractile-state closure is still needed before a long-time tissue envelope can be accepted. The parallel 1.01 pressure diagnosis remains untouched.
