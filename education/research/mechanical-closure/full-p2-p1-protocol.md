## Functional, pressure and true tangent

Use **Pi(u,p)=integral[Wmatrix+WpassiveFiber+Wactive+p log J−p²/(2K)] dV0**. Pressure p is in **Pa** and contributes **(p/J)I** to Cauchy stress. Its finite-K weak relation is **M p=K b(u)**, with **M_ij=integral Qi Qj dV0**, **b_i=integral Qi log J dV0**. Exactly eliminate this linear pressure equation at each objective evaluation, giving the genuine finite-P1 condensed energy **Enonvolume+(K/2)bᵀ M^-1 b**. This is equivalent to the discrete mixed saddle equations; it is not the pointwise displacement penalty when pressure is spatially nonuniform.
