## Conditional mechanical contract

For one selected implementation, C=Tref·Fscale [N] and ell=Lref/Gamma [m]. Smooth PE force prefactor is C·kpe/K, smoothing width ell/K, and asymptotic slope C·kpe/ell. SE prefactor is C·kse0 and inverse-length exponent kse/ell. Thus converting a raw kpe directly to N/m, or a raw kse0 directly to N, silently assigns missing scales. A printed stiffness label attached to a force prefactor cannot settle that conversion.

This elastic conversion is not a new constitutive law or a reconciliation of every source variant. The exact smooth PE coordinate re-expression must rescale width, onset and slope together; the entire piecewise runtime is outside this symbolic parity claim. Published Table3 and the raw summary script retain distinct provenance. Frozen initializer kappa examples at bc remain authored normalized cases, with no new identification of their value as a calibrated published coefficient.

Parallel head capacity H determines force H·kCB·dps·Q. Serial count Ns determines length and stored link energy while transmitting the same force. Neither a force/area ratio nor serial geometry identifies individual head count and stiffness. Tissue packing or overlap already included in the weighted population must not be counted again.

A declared local reference section gives nominal stress T/A0; its projected current fiber-normal area is J·A0/lambda, so Cauchy stress is lambda/J times nominal stress under the stated local force path. Whole-muscle unprojected PCSA and tendon-projected FCSA are separate quantities, with cosine applied once. Those identities require a declared configuration; they do not equate a mesh slice, tendon section, atlas fascicle or cohort PCSA.

## Verification and remaining mechanical gate

The [precommitted protocol](source-unit-calibration-protocol.md) declares an exact symbolic audit: 12 identities, four intentional dimensional rejections and two unit-consistent mechanical counterexamples. This checks the conditional algebra and integer SI exponents. It uses no independent numerical reference, fit, kinetic ODE, anatomy solver or machine-precision comparison and supplies no empirical validation. A unit-correct model can still violate force topology or the measured material response.

The current concrete physical blocker is the missing same-preparation specimen ledger linking calibrated force/stress, area/configuration, reference denominator, passive correction, length/sarcomere geometry and compatible elastic/model version. A cohort mean or assumed head scale does not fill that ledger. After those links are established, the next mechanical task is a predeclared coupled CE/PE/SE force-balance example with dimensional power and initialization checks, then tissue equilibrium and matched-time spatial/timestep refinement under the existing residual/geometry gates. No current evidence qualifies a self-consistent dense loaded arm trajectory or resolves bulk compression; no skin is admitted.
