# Original animal dataset candidates and acquisition limits

2026-10-06; research-only additions after frozen `306d0aba8e024086b9f4d04cc4d74f137b8b443a`. No numerical model, symbolic audit, parameter fitting or old evidence was changed. ## Best dynamic candidate: Altman2015 rabbit fibers

[Original paper](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0121726), Methods, describes permeabilized rabbit psoas fibers at5°C, sarcomere length set to2.5µm, camera-measured fiber length/diameter and circular-area estimation. Relaxed pCa9 and activated pCa4.5 sinusoidal trials are acquired, with separate inhibitor-treated trials. Mean subtraction of illustrated oscillations is not a documented subtraction of passive force from active force. The force transducer is Aurora400A; an individual sensor gain/zero calibration is not recovered from these Methods.

[Official Dryad data](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.jf35g) advertises18 Aurora600A DAT files, approximately326–333MB each, total5.92GB. Its usage notes identify paired conditions by fiber/date filenames; for example `DA_052212_f3_A.dat` is relaxed and `DA_052212_f3_B.dat` is activated before treatment. The record provides no separately advertised specimen-geometry table or README. That does not establish what is inside the uninspected DAT headers. No large download was made. Dryad's [terms §4](https://datadryad.org/terms) permit dataset reuse and describe a copyright waiver; the exact item-specific license badge was not independently extracted.

Smallest conditional experiment: retrieve just one A/B fiber pair with its actual channel calibration and paired diameter/length record, preserve the pCa9 baseline, and reproduce one5°C force–length oscillation and its area-normalized response. Choose a frequency/amplitude from that pair's actual trial metadata, not a paper cohort mean. Keep the rabbit-specific preparation and kinetics; do not import rat22°C rate medians. Before a force-scale fit, inspect the native channel/header specification and confirm whether displayed forces are total, baseline-subtracted, or normalized. The untreated paired records are preferable to inhibitor-treated records for that first comparison. **No numerical specimen is admitted here.**

## Best direct force-calibration candidate: Haeger2020 myofibrils

[Official Dryad archive](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.tht76hdwp) inventories a229.93KB force workbook, two3.05KB R scripts,15.10KB needle table,557.16KB sarcomere-tracking template,10.65KB test-values workbook, a33MB myofibril ZIP and a3.99MB single-sarcomere ZIP. Its Methods/Usage notes explicitly connect needle displacement tracks to force by a cross-bending-calibrated stiffness. This is a useful force metrology lead, not a complete matched record already obtained.

The [original publication](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2020.2133) publisher read failed internally; its PMC copy presented a browser challenge and was stopped. Species, temperature, activation, area, passive correction and per-preparation linkage are consequently **unverified here**. The ordinary small-file clicks returned internal errors. A single ordinary shell request to the official landing failed at the proxy tunnel with403; it was stopped. No needle table, workbook, ZIP or implementation was downloaded or executed.

Smallest conditional experiment: one single-sarcomere displacement trace joined to its identified needle-stiffness row and preparation geometry/activation metadata; reconstruct its force in N and compare its recorded length relation. This is smaller than processing the full myofibril archive. Missing calibration-row linkage and unverified preparation fields prevent selecting it now. Do not substitute the tracking template for an actual specimen track or infer area from a typical myofibril diameter.
