## What the Gejl check adds

The official PDF text repeats the label and table cells previously seen in HTML. For lateral-triceps MHC I/II, Table1 reports **7619±2566 / 8807±2142** under that label, with **n=27/48**. The force figure uses **n=28/60**. These are not identical assay subsets; a ratio of group mean force to group mean stress cannot reconstruct or verify the table's mean area.

Methods, printed p.3, derive cylindrical area from three diameters at straightened slack length, without swelling correction. The preparation paragraph specifies cold paraffin oil **0–5°C**; a distinct imaging temperature is not restated. Force recordings are **22.1±0.0°C**, pCa4.7, at **120% slack length** after a passive plateau. That plateau wait is not a measured relaxation time. The force/stress units are explicitly **mN** and **kN/m²**, respectively. These remain preparation-specific observations, not head-specific physiological optima.

No inspectable screenshot pixels or downloaded PDF bytes were available, so the repeated text label is **not** claimed as pixel verification or an established publisher typo. The PDF shell download was stopped at proxy tunnel403. The publisher's linked XML format was unavailable through the browser. No other download route, proxy change or restricted-content workaround was used. Plausible fiber magnitudes do not establish the intended unit. Original failure records and unconverted/unknown area fields remain intact.
