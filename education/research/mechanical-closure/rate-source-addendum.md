# Force-velocity implementation evidence and remaining rate closure

[OpenSim's original Thelen2003Muscle implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/opensim-org/opensim-core/main/OpenSim/Actuators/Thelen2003Muscle.cpp), inspected 2026-10-06, explicitly normalizes fiber length by optimal fiber length and velocity by optimal length times maximum contraction velocity. Active force combines activation, active force-length and force-velocity factors with pennation. Its dynamic muscle-tendon calculations distinguish contractile fiber length/velocity from tendon state; this is not a joint angular-velocity multiplier. The public `main` URL is mutable and was not imported or copied into this prototype.

The preserved Arm26 XML declares BICshort maximum speed **10 optimal fiber lengths/s**. This is a reference-model parameter, not a measured atlas-subject speed. [Saini and Röhrle's original musculotendon implementation paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169260722005338) likewise combines force-length/velocity with passive muscle/tendon behavior and characterized parameters. Neither source justifies inserting an arbitrary viscosity or damping value to pass a static curvature check.

Future dynamics must identify what fiber/reference configuration defines normalized rate, which series-elastic/contractile state carries it, and how force-velocity, activation and passive dissipation are calibrated separately. Preserve the specimen, temperature, fiber type and normalization limitations in [the measured-property map](../measured-fibre-property-map.md). A constitutive rate law changes the dynamical residual and physical Jacobian; it is not a positive surrogate Hessian. At zero rate it must reproduce the declared isometric law; under shortening/lengthening it needs independent force/work checks and matched timestep refinement. The present frozen diagnostic adds no rate term and establishes no dynamic stabilization.
