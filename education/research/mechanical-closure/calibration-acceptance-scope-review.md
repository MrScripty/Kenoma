# Calibration requirements depend on the claim

Review dated 2026-10-06, additive to frozen `c15b7f86ec1dfdccb55512c9a4ba3b1a1059b1dc`. This corrects the scope of the acceptance recommendations in [the data-search decision](matched-calibration-data-decision.md) and [the source-unit decision](source-unit-calibration-decision.md); their original receipts and numerical evidence remain intact. No dataset download, model run, physical default or constitutive equation changed in this review.

**The earlier recommendation unnecessarily made complete matched-specimen metrology a prerequisite for every dimensional mechanics experiment.** That standard belongs to a matched-specimen reproduction or a claim that parameters were measured together. Published calibrated force/stress, a source-backed reference-model parameter or an explicitly identified composite educational model can support a narrower executable experiment now.

## Minimum inputs versus stronger provenance

| Input | Mathematically necessary when | Sufficient evidence for a bounded experiment | Additional information needed for a stronger claim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Force unit/calibration | Assigning a dimensional force scale or converting a raw channel | Original study explicitly reports calibrated force in N or another convertible force unit; source-reported stress can directly scale a stress law | Raw gain/voltage calibration is needed if converting raw voltage or investigating calibration error; it is not automatically required to use published SI force |
| Area | Converting force to stress, or stress to resultant force | Source-defined reported specific force/stress, or an explicitly authored/model area for a virtual specimen | Actual paired area and configuration are needed to reconstruct a specimen's stress from its measured force; no area is needed merely to use a dimensional force scale |
| Reference configuration | Evaluating a finite-strain law or converting nominal/current stress | Declared virtual reference with the exact model stress measure; at F=I, J=1, nominal and Cauchy stress coincide | Measurement-pose/reference mapping is needed for a specimen or anatomical interpretation; source stress does not identify an atlas reference map |
| Length/sarcomeres | Converting normalized displacement or velocity into m or m/s; computing serial geometry | Existing declared virtual length and normalization can close a composite model | Individual sarcomere geometry is needed for a same-fiber serial-count claim; it is unnecessary for a static stress–stretch fixture |
| Activation/passive baseline | Interpreting a target as active rather than total force, or fitting activation dependence | Use the source's stated force convention, or compare total force with the complete declared model response; hold activation as an explicit model input | If decomposition is genuinely needed, unidentified passive force prevents identifying the active component; raw tare records are not required when the publication clearly defines subtraction |
| Temperature/preparation | Transferring rates, interpreting measured response or comparing protocols | Preserve source preparation/temperature labels; identify any composite transfer assumption | Same-preparation evidence is needed to validate transferred kinetics; a static educational amplitude test does not require a per-trial temperature trace |
| Individual pairing | Reproducing/fitting a specimen, reconstructing F/A, or claiming covariance | Published stress itself, separately identified cohort summaries, or source model constants can serve as deterministic sensitivity inputs | Matched rows are required to claim a measured joint specimen record; products/ratios of marginal means do not create one |
| Molecular head parameters | Claiming individual head density, stiffness or stroke as identified | A dimensional macro-force/stress scale can identify their aggregate product without separating every factor | Separate molecular evidence is needed only for separate parameter-identification claims |
| PE/SE and rate family | Running a particular coupled constitutive circuit | Complete, explicitly declared mathematical variant and dimensional scales; existing authored coefficients may be retained as educational assumptions | Compatible experiment records are needed to validate its passive, series and kinetic responses; unresolved implementation variants cannot be silently merged |

File hashes, versions, calibration certificates and detailed uncertainty ledgers improve provenance. Their absence does not make a published SI number mathematically unusable. Conversely, the undocumented processed numbers in the rat MAT files still cannot be called N or kPa merely because a related publication uses those units. This is a genuine unit-linkage gap, distinct from missing sensor coefficients.

## Four permissible scopes

1. **Implementation verification:** authored constants, declared geometry and reference conventions suffice to test force balance, derivatives, state evolution and refinement. Passing these tests does not validate the physical constants.
2. **Source-backed composite educational mechanics:** independently sourced amplitude, reference geometry and normalized kinetics may be combined if each transfer/assumption is explicit. The result must not be called a reproduction of any one source or a measured human parameter set.
3. **Study/specimen reproduction:** match the source quantity, preparation, geometry, force convention and actual selected record or reported aggregate observable. Raw calibration coefficients are needed only for calculations that use raw channels. A cohort observable can be reproduced without inventing individual rows.
4. **Anatomical/clinical validation:** requires independent observations appropriate to the claimed anatomy, load, response and uncertainty, beyond implementation verification or an educational composite. Numerical force/geometry/time gates remain indispensable at every scope involving a solve.

A cohort mean or published model coefficient can be a deterministic reference value. A pair of different fiber-type means can be sensitivity anchors. Neither is automatically a population interval, confidence bound, distribution or upper-arm property. Mean±SD can be reported as its source statistic without converting it into guaranteed patient limits.

## An important matched-force control

In the current instantaneous law the active term depends on **alpha=a·sigma0**. The preserved fixture uses sigma0=8,708,387.370104775Pa and a=.01, hence alpha=87,083.87370104775Pa. With all other inputs fixed, replacing sigma0 by a source anchor and choosing a=alpha/sigma0 produces exactly the same stress, tangent and equilibrium problem. Both155,000Pa and179,000Pa exceed that alpha, so the matched activations are within0–1. This is an algebraic observation, not a new solve or a physiological activation calibration.

Therefore an amplitude decrease at fixed a cannot be presented as a constitutive repair when it merely reduces carried force. At matched alpha, a peak-stress relabeling leaves the prior negative witnesses unchanged. The full-activation source-anchor run above asks a different, explicitly stated amplitude question. To change fast versus relaxed stiffness at matched force requires an actual internal-state/series formulation, not a different name for sigma0.

## Indispensable missing inputs and remaining uncertainty

**None is missing for the proposed composite static comparator.** Its dimensions, reference, law, source amplitude and numerical controls are all available. The new runner must pass explicit material/activation values to both implementations; do not depend on Python default arguments that were bound to the old activation. That is an implementation requirement, not missing biological data.

Still missing for a measured matched-fiber calibration are accessible selected rows and their actual quantity/configuration conventions. Still missing for a physiological transient are validated internal/series-state parameters and compatible rate/reference conventions. Still missing for the arm are a justified reference/architecture mapping, distributed force paths, bulk/passive response evidence and a self-consistent trajectory with spatial/quadrature/timestep qualification. These restrict the claims that may follow; they do not block the proposed feature-level experiment.

This review executes no simulation and changes no book/Lean/constitutive equation. Existing failures, numerical tolerances, protected scope/figure files and the frozen MMU research candidate remain unchanged.
