# Owner-approved bounded Filippov experiment

This is a separate scalar research experiment, authorized after review of [the local analysis](antiwindup-continuation-analysis.md) and [the protocol clarifications](antiwindup-continuation-protocol-clarifications.md). The approval permits testing the explicitly declared Filippov convention; it does not adopt it into the book or qualify anatomical mechanics. The original ten-full/two-prefix trajectories and all files at 9ab5f9c629a3c200c1c4edf0fadc250a384eb78f remain immutable.

## Declared fields and independent representations

At a single strictly attracting, outward-error surface, the research field is

\[
 \dot p=G(p,B),\qquad \dot I=-k_p\dot e,
 \qquad \theta=-k_p\dot e/(k_i e)\in(0,1).
\]

The two mechanical limiting fields agree at excitation bound \(B\). No gain, curve, activation law, velocity-root bracket/iteration limit or mechanical force law changes. The integral is an information coordinate, not mechanical storage; the original force, component work and impulse identities apply. Strict signs and source validity remain necessary. There is no new exit policy through zero error, a zero normal, command jumps or a failed physical probe. These conditions and the distinction from finite-gain back-calculation are derived in the reviewed analysis, based on [Dieci–Lopez's primary paper](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/080724599) and [Cortés's author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0901.3583).

The full five-state representation calls the **actual immutable JavaScript source kernels** through [a research adapter](../../tools/filippov_source_worker.mjs). On sliding only, the adapter evaluates the original activation formula at \(u=B\) without modifying \(I\). The separate reduced representation uses the existing independent BPoly/Brent kernels, advances four mechanical coordinates on sliding and reconstructs \(I=B-u_b-k_p e\). Both representations retain five coordinates and their own controller history before entry. Neither consumes the other's event time, state, trajectory or ledger.

[The runner](../../tools/run_filippov_bounded_experiment.py) exercises the full reviewed matrix: both representations, RK4 .0002/.0001/.00005 s, DOP853 and Radau, for high and mass-1. The fixed endpoints are .107/.264 s respectively, with original initializations. Adaptive settings remain rtol 1e−9, atol 1e−11 and maximum step .0005 s. Radau uses fixed physical-column differences for its Jacobian, with exactly zero ledger columns, avoiding the retained earlier quadrature-column finite-difference overflow. This is a numerical implementation choice, not an added damping or controller parameter.

All ten candidate entries in a case are independently localized **before any sliding entry is accepted**. The original physical gates, 2 µs event gate, 32-iteration/1e−7 s bracket criterion and the predeclared 1e−9 integral, 1e−8 raw and 1e−9 constraint diagnostics remain unchanged. A case failing these common entry checks retains each cell's last pre-entry accepted state; the candidate and auxiliary suffix are evidence only. A completed localization is not a completed bounded trajectory.

## Incoming localization, ordinary crossings and preservation

The one-sided integrating auxiliary is \((G(p,\operatorname{clip}(b,.01,1)),k_i e)\); excitation clipping remains active throughout its probes. RK4 uses its stage-based continuous extension; adaptive methods use their actual dense polynomials. Root bracketing follows that incoming extension, with valid physical stages and no mechanical projection or integral reset. Beyond-surface stages, roots under consideration and discarded suffixes are stored separately, excluded from accepted history and work/impulse ledgers. Gaussian quadrature evaluates only accepted portions of those actual method polynomials, using independent curve/root powers. Invalid physical probes stop and retain the preceding accepted state; they are not hidden by smaller steps.

The first implementation stopped mass-1's earlier **ordinary** outward crossing as an unsupported event, before reaching the opposing surface. Its entire twenty-cell run, sources, raw failures, audit and renders are [preserved separately](../../data/filippov-bounded-v1/review/pre-ordinary-crossing-fix/packet-manifest.json). It is unqualified evidence, not a sliding solution. Its matrix source is 213cca56ac73e9a2289cd5c4016903267b805482; the first audit source is d3f89638875473f1bde702acbe670c326f9c49ba. Receipts retain their original path names; the preservation manifest maps those files to their retained subtree and binds executed source snapshots.

The corrected runner handles these existing ordinary crossings by localizing the incoming branch, accepting only its valid prefix, then selecting the original frozen or integrating branch when **both normals certify the same crossing direction**. It does not interpret an ordinary crossing as sliding. A frozen incoming extension keeps \(\dot I=0\) and the original excitation clipping solely for inward-crossing localization. Error-direction changes not covered by this bounded construction stop explicitly. Event count/type/timing comparisons include these ordinary crossings as well as the attracting candidate. This correction changes neither an off-surface controller branch nor a mechanical field. The corrected matrix source is 034d2c8115ed5eff338b77f0e75bbfe036f6f3a8, tree a10f88379ad697acd33350eb8d5d08c456c62c13.

## Results and limits

**Neither case qualifies.** All twenty cells independently localized an attracting candidate; both cases failed the required common-time controller diagnostics. All twenty retained their last pre-entry accepted state with `entry-refinement-block`. There are **zero accepted sliding entries, zero post-surface accepted states and zero completed bounded trajectories**. The implemented sliding stepping paths consequently have no numerical trajectory qualification from this packet.

The 2 µs attracting-event disagreement criterion passes in both cases. Mass-1's ordinary outward/inward crossings occur near .107883130/.249882721 s in the finest source RK4 run; their ten-cell time spans are 6.104/30.518 ns, also below the unchanged gate. All root probes meet the 32-iteration/1e−7 s bracket and 1e−9 surface-residual criteria: maximum widths are 3.052 ns (high) and 97.656 ns (mass-1), with at most 19/12 iterations respectively. The three lower RK4 candidate times coincide at the localization resolution; that does **not** establish an asymptotic event-convergence order. Candidate/event rows remain auxiliary evidence, not accepted orbit points.

The high integral witness is at .059 s (source coarse RK4 versus independent Radau); its raw witness is at .051 s. The lower integral witness is at .250 s (independent coarse RK4 versus source finest RK4); its raw witness is at .228 s. Absolute-time adjacent RK4 integral differences are 4.0793e−9 then 2.6039e−10 for high, and 3.6545e−9 then 2.6155e−9 for mass-1. The first high refinement ratio is about 15.7, consistent with a smooth fourth-order truncation-error diagnosis; the lower ratio is about 1.4 and does not demonstrate asymptotic controller convergence. These are measured diagnostics, not extrapolated accepted states or a justification to omit a required level.

A candidate contributor to the lower order loss is the **existing** activation branch change at excitation equal to activation: the finest lower accepted history changes the sign of `u−a` between .002 and .003 s. The two source time constants give different one-sided derivatives while the activation rate itself is continuous at equality. This sampled bracket is not a localized activation event and does not establish causal attribution. No activation-event modification, smoothing or constitutive change was made to pursue a pass.

All twenty **accepted prefixes** pass independent force, physical-bound, component/combined work, impulse and actual-dense-polynomial quadrature checks. Across them, the peak stage force-root residual is 9.9995e−11 N (gate 1e−7 N), peak accepted independent force closure is 1.4886e−12 N, peak work-ledger error is 2.4571e−9 J (gate 1e−5 J), peak impulse-ledger error is 9.7145e−17 N*s and peak independent dense impulse disagreement is 2.2137e−11 N*s (gate 1e−7 N*s). These incoming-prefix certificates do **not** certify sliding work, mechanical impulses at entry or completion of either short endpoint.

Upper-surface candidate timing alone is excellent: the first and corrected runs give a span of about .326 ns across the ten cells. Nonetheless, the coarse RK4 command transient fails the stricter predeclared diagnostics: maximum common-grid integral difference 4.3571749e−9 exceeds 1e−9, and raw-signal difference 4.1571196e−8 exceeds 1e−8. The runner blocks entry rather than limiting the diagnostics to a later interval, dropping the coarse cell, changing its step or aligning trajectories. Fine/finer or adaptive agreement cannot qualify a matrix with a required failing cell.

The lower surface remains weakly transverse. The reviewed 22.4 µs sensitivity calculation from the allowed force error is a diagnostic, not an error bound or event certificate. Stricter integral/raw checks and narrow root brackets do not replace independently refined event-time evidence. Physical force/work consistency likewise cannot override failed controller/event refinement or missing endpoint coverage.

[The independent audit](../../tools/audit_filippov_bounded_experiment.py) compares adjacent RK4 levels, both adaptive methods, the finest RK4 against each adaptive method and matching methods across representations, using absolute timestamps. It checks each required endpoint and grid, event count/type/time, independent source-force closure, physical bounds, each component/combined ledger, impulse, independent dense quadrature, controller differences and normal/constraint/tangency consistency where sliding is accepted. Prefix balance passes are explicitly separate from full completion. Negative controls cover actual source slack probes, failure rollback, invalid brackets, zero-error sliding, constraint drift, missing cells, sparse/truncated completion and event/integral disagreement.

The outstanding blocker is numerical evidence under the complete predeclared matrix and gates. No gate or controller can be changed merely to turn this experiment into a pass. Any different matrix or diagnostic scope requires a separately reviewed protocol decision; these results do not authorize one. No anatomical, global-continuation or descending-stability claim follows, and no skin or book/Lean adoption is made.
