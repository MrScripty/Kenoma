# Restore the original derivative gate scope

The first amplitude execution is preserved in `review/source-amplitude-p2-p1`. Its 1.01 case correctly fails pointwise stationary pressure compatibility. Its 1.25 case passes all original stationarity/replay gates and both independent gradient-relative-error checks, but the new wrapper additionally required pointwise pressure compatibility at arbitrary nonstationary eigenvector probes. That extra requirement was not in the original derivative verifier.

Exact original implementation: [verify_full_p2_p1.py](../../tools/verify_full_p2_p1.py), gradient-probe loop. It requires independent assembly difference<=2e-6N and weak pressure RMS<=1e-6, then the original relative gradient gate<=1e-4. It records side J and pointwise pressure diagnostics without requiring pointwise RMS<=1e-6 at the probes. Accepted equilibria still require both weak and pointwise pressure RMS<=1e-6. The physical condensed operator is differentiated after eliminating finite-P1 pressure; arbitrary perturbed P2 fields need not have pointwise logJ represented in P1.

At coarse1.25 the two relative errors are3.4087591e-10/5.3285049e-10. The1e-7m probe has pointwise RMS1.6854e-6/1.6865e-6; these explain the wrapper's additional rejection. The terminal equilibrium RMS is6.4792890e-8. Its force/geometry/pressure/replay gates remain unchanged.

The successor [source_amplitude_p2_p1_v2.py](../../tools/source_amplitude_p2_p1_v2.py) restores the original derivative scope. It reads the original coarse receipts and reclassifies the retained probes without repeating a coarse solve or changing any number. The first classification remains intact. It then refines only the now-qualified coarse1.25 case on the original8×2×2 mesh. Coarse1.01 stays rejected and its fine request remains skipped. Use the exact existing budgets, coefficients, perturbed start, quadratures, force/pressure/geometry/assembly/work/refinement and derivative tolerances; no gate is relaxed from the original comparator.

Fine derivative probes receive the original assembly/weak-pressure/relative-gradient checks, with positive finite geometry and the existing sampled geometry/crossing guards. Pointwise pressure at probes is recorded as a diagnostic. Fine accepted equilibria retain all pointwise pressure gates. The successor writes exclusively into `review/source-amplitude-p2-p1-v2` and preserves all failures. This is a gate-scope correction, not a solver retry or a changed material law.
