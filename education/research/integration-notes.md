## Required corrections during later chapter integration

- Count each gravitational load once: either include gravity in the multibody bias term or in the applied generalized force, with the convention stated.
- Takaza et al. (2013) is orientation-dependent passive **tensile** evidence from porcine tissue. It cannot validate transverse human-arm compression.
- First Piola-Kirchhoff and Cauchy stresses both have Pa units. Confusing them changes the stress measure and its reference/current configuration interpretation.
- Reconcile angle conventions explicitly: this lever's angle θ is from horizontal, while a downward-zero elbow flexion coordinate q gives θ = q - π/2.
- Define contact force f as the force **on the bone**, with -f on tissue, before transferring moments.
- Use direct tissue embedding or a residual against a baseline at the **same current pose and frame**. Adding unposed rest-relative tissue displacement to already posed skinning double-counts pose.
- A tetrahedral six-edge spring/constraint network is not a tetrahedral finite element. Define volume, material energy, deformation gradient, force and inversion behavior independently.
- Joint reaction resultants, anterior skin contact and posterior fat-pad motion are different outputs. Penetration is not pressure.
- Assign one mechanical owner for each active muscle so a line actuator and an active continuum do not double-count force/work.
