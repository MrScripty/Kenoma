The declared map and its limits

λ(S) = λ̄ [1 + a(2S/L₀ − 1)]
x(S) = λ̄ [(1 − a)S + aS²/L₀]
y = b(S)Y,  z = b(S)Z
b(S) = 1/√λ(S) with compensation; b = 1 otherwise

F = [ λ      0  0 ]
    [ b′Y    b  0 ]
    [ b′Z    0  b ]
J = det F = λb²

λ > 0 makes the axial map monotone. Compensation gives J = 1 for the exact continuum map. The off-diagonal terms b′Y and b′Z describe the additional shear away from the centerline; a nonuniform stretch is not a sequence of unrelated uniform blocks. The reported engineering strain λ − 1 is the axial-line strain at Y = Z = 0.

With compensation off and mean stretch one, total volume is still unchanged because expansion and contraction cancel. Local J then differs from one. A near-unity total-volume number alone therefore does not establish local volume preservation.

Finite boundary triangles approximate the exact curved map. Their volume error is reported and should decrease with refinement. These geometric results do not establish a passive equilibrium, a muscle law, pressure, or anatomical completion.
